`tix.Note(id, body)` in events.go: reject blank bodies, confirm the ticket
exists, `recordEvent(db, id, "note", {"body": body})`. No status or
`updated_at` change; notes are history, not transitions. `tix.Notes(id)`
filters `Events` to note events for show.

`cmd/note.go` mirrors `reviewOutcome`: resolve ref, project match, call
`tix.Note`. Body comes from the second arg or `--body-file`, not both.

Plain `tix show` prints a `notes:` section after the body; `--json` stays the
ticket (notes are in `--events --json`). MCP gets `tix_note` and `tix_show`
appends notes. Agent guidance gains one line: leave `tix note` when stopping
without `tix done`. The user's `next-ticket` skill (outside this repo) gets
the same handoff step.
